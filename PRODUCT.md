# PacketScope
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
SOC analysts, network engineers and incident responders investigating locally
collected packet captures. The full supplied specification establishes this brief.

## Product Purpose
Turn observed network traffic into navigable, evidence-backed investigations.
An analyst, not an automated score, determines the incident verdict.

## Operating Context
Local workstation, browser interface, offline PCAP and PCAPNG analysis. No active
scanning, captured-payload execution or external evidence sharing by default.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Immutable SHA-256 capture identity, frame references, bidirectional flows,
observable protocol metadata, deterministic indicators, cases and reports.
Encrypted or missing traffic must never be represented as known content.

## Product Principles
Correctness before breadth. Evidence before claims. Explicit limits before silent
truncation. Core investigations must remain usable without integrations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-accessible controls, readable contrast, responsive layout, meaningful
loading, empty and failure states.

## Evidence on Hand
Only generated benign fixtures. No customer captures, reputation data or
commercial claims are available.
